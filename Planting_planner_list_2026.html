📝 Notes

Ensure you plant a large quantity of Lomandra species. Plant at least 1 metre from a driveway or path.

Native plants in gardens do not tend to reach their full height. Keep specimens well pruned to ensure height is restricted if necessary.

The vines shown on this list are mostly "scramblers". Fast-growing, large woody vines are not recommended in gardens or new plantings as they may overwhelm immature trees and bring them down.

Ensure you plant pioneer species such as Poison Peach, Native Mulberry and Native Hibiscus.

Other plants to keep away from driveways and paths include figs, Peanut Tree, Alectryon and other trees which fruit heavily or with large woody fruit.
